Questions

Do I need to install an app?

No. The video call opens in your browser right here on this website. Just allow camera and microphone access when asked. No WhatsApp video, no third-party apps.

How do I join my video consultation?

After booking, the consultation link is sent to your WhatsApp. At your appointment time, open it, enter your name and wait a moment. Dr. Vigeesh will let you in. You can also tap "Join video call" at the top of this page.

How do I pay?

When you tap Book, you'll see the fee and a UPI QR code. Pay with any UPI app, enter the transaction reference, and then choose your time.

How will I get my medicines after an online consultation?

Dr. Vigeesh will explain your remedies at the end of the call. Medicines can be collected from the clinic in Pattambi, or the clinic will tell you how they can reach you.

Is homoeopathy suitable for children and older adults?

Yes. The clinic offers holistic care for all ages, with doses and advice suited to each person.

Is my consultation private?

Yes. Calls happen in a private room on this website, and only you and the doctor are present.
